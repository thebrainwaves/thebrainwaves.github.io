Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This site is run by Kyle Carlin, Brainhertz Productions, Los Angeles, California. Contact: brainhertzproductions@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or our own cookies. The site is static and stores nothing about you. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address and browser details to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Third-party content

Some parts of the site load content from other companies, and only when you choose to:

  • Spotify players load only after you click "Load Spotify player". Spotify may then set cookies and collect usage data (Spotify privacy policy).
  • The YouTube music video loads only after you click it, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The preview image comes from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com), which receives your IP address (Google privacy policy).
  • Links to Instagram, YouTube, Spotify and other sites open those sites, which have their own policies.

Niche' (software)

Buying or activating Niche' will involve payment and license data. A separate privacy notice for Niche' will be published before it goes on sale.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have the right to access or delete personal data we hold about you. Email us and we will help.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
