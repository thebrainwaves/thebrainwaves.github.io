What Hive does

  • A personality of your own

    Tune how Hive speaks, thinks and responds until it feels like yours.

  • Encrypted memory that stays home

    Shared memory is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stays on your machine.

  • A hive mind

    A coordinator brings in specialist agents, and an independent verifier checks the work before it reaches you.

  • A live neuron dashboard

    Watch the hive at work on a live hexagon dashboard as each agent lights up.

  • Debate mode

    The agents argue a question, a judge scores them, and you get a final answer with the dissent shown.

  • Collaborate mode

    The agents build one answer together and agree on it.

  • Local-first

    Runs locally through Ollama and routes to Grok when it's needed.

  • Voice chat

    Talk to Hive out loud and hear it answer.
