Niche' 1.0

Simple Means Smarter

A VJ and projection-mapping app for Windows. Launch clips, stack layers, drive effects from audio, and map visuals onto any surface.

Built by Kyle Carlin at Brainhertz Productions and inspired by AI.

Features

Some features depend on the version; see Buy below.

  • Unlimited layers and clips

    Stack as many layers and load as many clips as your machine can handle. Included in Standard and Full.

  • Effects and FFGL plugins

    A built-in effects library, plus support for FFGL plugins.

  • Projection mapping

    Map output onto screens, objects and architecture, with warping and edge blending.

  • Spout and NDI

    Send and receive video between apps and machines over Spout and NDI.

  • Audio-reactive input

    Drive effects and parameters from live audio input.

  • Theme editor

    Change the interface colours and layout to suit your booth and your eyes.

FAQ

What kind of license is it?

Every version is a perpetual license. You pay once and keep using Niche'. See the EULA for the full terms.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Move from Lite to Standard or Full, or from Standard to Full, at any time and pay only the difference in price.

Are updates free?

Yes. Every version includes free updates.

How does activation work?

After purchase you receive a license key by email. Paste it into Niche' to activate and the trial watermark is removed. Card payments send the key automatically; Zelle payments send it once the payment is approved.

Does it run on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Niche' is Windows-only for now (Windows 10 or 11).

Are there student and teacher discounts?

Yes. Students get 75% off and teachers get 100% off. Both are verified by hand; use the request form above.

What is the refund policy?

See the refund policy. Please try the free trial first to make sure Niche' works on your hardware.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The trial unlocks every feature from every version, with a "Niche' - Unlicensed" watermark on the output until you activate a key.
